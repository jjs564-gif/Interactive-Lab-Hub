# NEON RACE | Video Demonstration

## Multiplayer Interaction

[Watch the NEON RACE Multiplayer Demonstration](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11VT5Zs120NAuV9nys2I1SqoBgyaAqhUp/view?usp=sharing)

This video documents a working multiplayer interaction between two Raspberry Pis using MQTT messaging and sensor-based challenges.

The original design included three Raspberry Pis. During integration testing, one teammate experienced a PiTFT display malfunction that prevented full participation. With approval from DTA Albert, we demonstrated the working two-player version.

The demonstration captures the distributed interaction, including the physical sensor inputs, network communication, and synchronized game behavior.
